Not a checklist you finish once. A loop you run every time you ship something.

What is it, exactly?

Demonstrated Competence

Code you shipped + the causal story of why you shipped it that way.

It's not a personality trait. It's a skill you build over time — not something you wake up with.

Why now?

Good Code Was Never the Whole Job

Today's market doesn't just ask "can you build it?"

It asks "can you explain why?"

On-the-Day Checklist

Before you take the stage

Laptop charged 50%+, offline copy of the deck, adapters/dongles packed, phone + laptop on Do Not Disturb, badge off, pockets empty, bathroom before you go up.

During

Presentation remote, not a laser pointer. Water on stage. End on "Thank you" — let the organizer run Q&A so the room knows when to clap.

If a demo or poll fails

Screenshots are already on Slides 10/25/28 as a fallback. Laugh it off, keep moving — don't troubleshoot live.
