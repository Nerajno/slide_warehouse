02 · Make A Plan

Schedule Like a Pro

Do This
  • Lock your primary sessions early
  • Have backup sessions ready (rooms fill up)
  • Block time for the hallway track
  • Read the conference emails — all of them
  • Check for after-hours events (they fill fast)

"Leave time in your schedule to talk with people, visit booths, and decompress."

— Abbey Perini

The hallway is a session track.

03 · Pack Right

The Gear Checklist

Bring

  • Laptop / tablet + charger
  • Portable battery pack
  • Notebook + pens (backup always)
  • Conference app downloaded
  • Comfortable shoes

Don't Forget

  • Water bottle — hydration is non-negotiable
  • Snacks (best conf might still run out)
  • Semi-casual, comfortable clothes
  • QR code or stickers to share contact info
  • Business cards or digital equivalent
Travel is hard Plan like the venue won't feed you Dress for YOU

04 · Network

Without the Cringe

  1. Have your LinkedIn QR ready — one tap, connected

  2. Collect stickers, pins, swag — instant conversation starters

  3. It's a small world — be kind, you'll most likely see them again

  4. Introduce yourself even if you've met — no pressure to remember

05 · Take Notes

Capture Insights, Not Transcripts

What to Capture

Key takeaways · actionable points · contacts mentioned · resources to look up · new tech to explore

How to Do It

Cornell method · note-taking app · voice memos · main idea + 3 bullets per talk

Focus on summarizing the main ideas — not transcribing every word. Your notes should be actionable, not a novel.

— @Nerajno, AttendeeEdition

07 · After the Conference

The Work Doesn't Stop Here

This Week
  • Reach out to people you met on LinkedIn
  • Engage with their content — not just a connect
  • Write the blog post on your experience
  • Share what you learned publicly
Next 30 Days
  • Build something with a new tech you discovered
  • Try out that tool from the sponsor booths
  • Submit a CFP for the next conference
  • Join a local meetup you heard about there

Progress over perfection. Ship the blog post. Connect with the person. Submit the talk idea.
