Act 1 / GEO Explained

What GEO actually is

GEO is

The practice of structuring content so AI engines can ingest, understand, and cite it in generated answers.

GEO isn't

SEO with a new name. It's a different discovery mechanism with its own goals, metrics, and technical needs.

1:55–2:55 (1:00). Land the "isn't" side hardest — that's the misconception most of the room walked in with.
Act 1 / GEO Explained

SEO vs. GEO — the differences that matter

Discovery

SEO: ranked links on a results page. GEO: synthesized into one generated answer.

Content focus

SEO: keywords and backlinks. GEO: explicit, self-contained, structured statements.

Metrics

SEO: rank position and clicks. GEO: citation frequency and conversational reach.

Why AI needs structure to cite you

Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, and AI Overviews don't read your page the way a person does — they extract discrete, self-contained claims they can lift out of contextcitable.

Vague, "as mentioned above" writing is invisible to a model that only sees one paragraph at a time.
